FAQ

How do I order?

Browse the menu and tap the ♡ on what you like. Open My Picks and tap Check out, or give us a call at (401) 753-9860. We’ll confirm your order.

Do I pay on the website?

No. This site is a menu only and doesn’t take payments. We confirm your order by call or text.

Is the total on My Picks final?

It’s an estimate based on the menu prices. We’ll confirm the final total with you.

Who can order?

You must be 21 or older.

What’s the free pre-roll deal?

Every 10th purchase earns a free pre-roll. Create a free account on the Rewards page, give us your card ID when you buy, and we add a stamp to your card. It follows you on any device.

Can I add this to my home screen?

Yes. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Add to Home screen.

Can I save your contact?

Yes. Open the Contact page and tap Save our contact, or tap Show QR code and scan it with your phone camera.

Are my picks saved?

Yes, on the device you’re using. Clearing your browser data removes them.
