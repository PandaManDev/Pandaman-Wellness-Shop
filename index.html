FAQ

How do I order?

Browse the menu and tap the ♡ on what you like. Open My Picks and tap Text my picks, or give us a call at (401) 753-9860. We’ll confirm your order.

Do I pay on the website?

No. This site is a menu only and doesn’t take payments. We confirm your order by call or text.

Is the total on My Picks final?

It’s an estimate based on the menu prices. We’ll confirm the final total with you.

Who can order?

You must be 21 or older.

What’s the free pre-roll deal?

Every 10th purchase earns a free pre-roll. See the Rewards page.

Can I add this to my home screen?

Yes. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Add to Home screen.

Are my picks saved?

Yes, on the device you’re using. Clearing your browser data removes them.
